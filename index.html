What is Postie CRM Optimization?

Your CRM holds the answers. Postie finds them.

Your CRM contains years of transaction data, behavioral signals, and campaign history — but turning all of that into meaningful targeting decisions on a continuous basis is nearly impossible at scale. With Postie CRM Optimization, we do the learning for you.

Powered by deep reinforcement learning, our machine learning engine transforms your existing first-party data into precision direct mail targeting that goes far beyond static, rules-based segmentation. Rather than sorting customers into broad buckets like "lapsed" or "VIP," it surfaces hidden segments your team didn't know existed — separating frequent buyers from high-AOV buyers, identifying fence-sitters who need a nudge, and flagging organic buyers who'd convert without a mailer so your budget isn't wasted on them.

The model doesn't just describe what happened in the past — it predicts timing, purchase intent, and incremental lifetime value for each customer, then continuously adapts as behavior evolves. Every campaign runs with a holdout control group to measure true incremental lift, and those results feed directly back into the model. The outcome is a CRM program that compounds — delivering progressively higher incremental revenue, lower CPAs, and stronger ROAS with every send.

In Practice, This Means:

1

Identify a missing purchase window that exists for an individual and trigger a direct mail piece at the right time for a conversion.

2

Identify high and consistent spenders to exclude from campaigns because they're unlikely to make additional purchases.

3

Remove low or non-converting individuals from an audience list who shouldn't be mailed during a particular campaign.

4

Dig deep into your data to find people on the cusp of spending more frequently and add them to cadences that will increase LTV.
